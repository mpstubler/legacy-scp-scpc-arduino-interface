# What am I looking at?

A proof-of-concept for adding external control to a retired Sorin/Stöckert SCP/SCPC perfusion pump (heart and lung machine).

## Why would someone want to do that?

Mainly for ex vivo perfusion research (organ preservation for transplants), although the same idea could be useful for other perfusion or ECMO bench work.

Instead of someone sitting there adjusting the pump and writing down measurements, the pump can respond automatically to measured variables while the experiment logs the data.

It also lets otherwise useful clinical hardware be upcycled instead of thrown away. Decommissioned systems sometimes show up surprisingly cheap on eBay and other secondary markets.

## Is this a finished project?

Not really.

The SCP/SCPC is uncommon and 2 generations behind current systems. The point was never this specific pump.

The point was to show that reverse-engineering a retired clinical pump and adding modern external control is practical enough to be worth doing again on newer, more common hardware.

## What is in this repository?

Code, wiring, notes, demos, and enough documentation to follow what was done.

**Research use only. Not intended for clinical use.**
