# What am I looking at?

A proof-of-concept for adding external control to a retired Sorin/Stöckert SCP/SCPC perfusion pump (heart-lung machine).

## Why would someone want to do that?

Mainly for ex vivo perfusion research, such as organ preservation for transplantation, although the same approach could be useful for other perfusion or ECMO bench work.

Instead of someone sitting at the pump adjusting flow and recording measurements, the pump can respond automatically to measured variables while the experiment logs the data.

It also creates a way to reuse otherwise functional clinical hardware after retirement. Older perfusion systems and components are often used on eBay and similar at prices within grant limits.

## Is this a finished project?

No. It's a proof-of-concept. This pump was never the end goal.

The SCP/SCPC is relatively uncommon and two generations behind current systems. The point was never this specific pump.

The goal was to show that reverse-engineering a retired clinical pump and adding modern external control is practical enough to justify doing it again on newer, more common hardware.

## What is in this repository?

Code, wiring, notes, demos, and enough documentation to follow what was done and reproduce the general approach.

**Research use only. Not intended for clinical use.**
